I bring home the impression that, upon closer inspection, much of the aura of the unknown with which we in Germany are accustomed to cloaking our eastern neighbour does indeed dissolve, but that we will nonetheless experience much from Russia in the future, both politically and economically, the anticipation of which already compels us today to take up a position toward our eastern neighbour that, wherever our interests collide across the globe, can be nothing other than the opposite of “laisser faire, laisser aller”.
