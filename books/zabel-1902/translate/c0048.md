The conditions in Turkestan are, of course, different for us than in Mongolia and Manchuria. Nevertheless, it is characteristic that Russia is permitted to sever from the torso of China a territory approximately the size of non-Russian Europe, and that the German press claims this does no harm, that they are merely "vassal states." We shall see where our policy of "laisser faire, laisser aller" toward Russia in East Asia will lead us. The answer to the question of whether the Russo-Chinese treaty has already received its ratification or not, or ever will receive it, is fundamentally quite immaterial. For it is no longer a question of whether this formality has been or will be fulfilled, but rather of the fact that Russia *is already de facto in the exercise of the rights demanded in the draft contract—has entered into these rights!*

Even the first article of the agreement contains a sheer sham provision: "Since the Emperor of Russia wishes to express his friendly feelings toward China, he agrees that Manchuria shall be completely restored to China, that the recent warlike events in that province shall be paid [[p106]] no further heed, and that the Chinese administration shall in every respect re-enter the status quo ante." What is that supposed to mean: "Manchuria is completely restored to China," when Russia is allowed to keep military forces permanently along the railway line; when China is not permitted to raise an army in Manchuria again until the railway is completed, and then only with the approval of Russia, which determines its strength; when even the Chinese police forces to be maintained in the province are subject to Russia's supreme control; when China is permitted to employ in the northern provinces no nationals of other states, but only Russians, as military instructors; when the local authorities on the Liaodong Peninsula are placed under Russia's strict control; when China is not permitted, without Russia's consent, to grant economic concessions of any kind to nationals of other states in the northern provinces, indeed not even the acquisition or leasing of land outside Newchwang; and when, though it is not in the treaty, but is how it is to be handled, Russia places the administration of the Chinese government officials under the supervision and direct control of Russians? It is obvious that under these circumstances the severance of Manchuria from China, even if not a formal one, is nonetheless a de facto one, and that it is a farce under such conditions to emphasize that Manchuria is being restored fully and entirely to China. This first article is evidently not calculated for China, but is intended to influence the foreign powers, vis-à-vis whom they wish to deny that Russia has annexed Manchuria. It would likewise be a farce if Russia were to withdraw its troops from Manchuria. In that case it would simply reinforce its railway guards accordingly, and that these are to be withdrawn has never been spoken of and never will be spoken of.

The system for acquiring Manchuria is, of course, an extraordinarily convenient and cheap one. China provides the experienced bureaucratic apparatus, pays all administrative costs out of its own pocket, and is liable with all its possessions for any damage inflicted on the Russians in any way in Manchuria, yet the country is governed exclusively by Russians and exclusively for Russians. With this, however, the provinces of Manchuria, Mongolia, and Chinese Turkestan are removed from the so-called Open Door policy, and in addition China is still liable for damage inflicted on Russia by Chinese within a part of Russia! What more could one ask for? A politically well-thought-out and profitable plan! Surely, however, in the face of these facts, one will no longer be able to close one's eyes to the insight that through such a separate treaty the economic strength of China is weakened in the most sensitive manner to the detriment of the country itself and of the other powers who deal with China purely on a commercial-policy basis. But that was precisely what the Anglo-German Agreement was intended to prevent!
