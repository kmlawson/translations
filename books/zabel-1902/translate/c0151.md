### Deutschland in China [Germany in China]

By

Rudolf Zabel

Large 8vo. XVI and 433 pp. Price paperbound Mk. 7.50, bound Mk. 9.—

The book, which falls into two main parts, represents a gratifying achievement. First, the policy of the powers toward China, Russia's tendency toward territorial acquisition, and, in contrast to this, the commercial practice of the other powers, are discussed from a strictly historical-juridical standpoint. Then, however, the author, who took part in the campaign as a German war correspondent, reports his own experiences and the results of his exploratory travels in Manchuria, Shandong, and South China, with other observations also being taken into account and addressed. Thus the actual history of the campaign is given further color through vivid presentation and firsthand observation, in particular through the departure, the first actions, and the occupation of the province of Zhili in its full extent and development. All kinds of East Asian questions, answers, and further problems form the instructive supplement. The book is practical, clear, and well-organized, and will thoroughly dispel many erroneous accounts that have circulated since then. *Münchener Allgemeine Zeitung* [Munich General Newspaper], November 11, 1902.
