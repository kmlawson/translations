### Dreiunddreißig Jahre in Ost-Asien [Thirty-Three Years in East Asia]

Memoirs of a German Diplomat

By M. von Brandt
Actual Privy Councillor, Imperial Envoy (ret.)

3 volumes. With a frontispiece in each.
Large 8vo. Price stitched Mk. 19.50, bound Mk. 24.—

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

. . . . One may rightly lend an ear to the words of a man who has lived for decades in the Far East and must therefore know the land and its people intimately: much of what he says must help to clarify views. Now that Herr von Brandt has decided to publish the results and experiences of his long official career, he can be assured of the attention of a large readership. Hamburger Nachrichten [Hamburg News].
