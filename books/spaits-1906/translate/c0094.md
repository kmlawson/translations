On the morning of 5 March, Japanese patrols were already appearing north of Mukden; on that day, the partial evacuation of the Mukden railway station and the dispatch to the rear of the Mukden post office began, the latter subsequently establishing itself in Kuanchengzi.

[[p313]]

Once again—*for the fourth time in a short period*—a *general reserve* had to be formed; Colonel Tsekhovich, chief of the topographical section which had now become idle, rode around to the higher headquarters to take away from them as many dispensable (?) troops as possible!

South of the Hun River, between it and the Sha River, stood General von der Launitz with elements of the 5th Siberian, 8th Army, and elements of the Rifle Corps, together with artillery.

The center and the First Army generally held their ground in their positions.

#### 6 and 7 March.

During the night of the 5th/6th, the 31st Brigade of the combined division of the 10th Corps was alerted and marched at 3:00 a.m. to the south bank of the Hun River, where it arrived at 7:00 a.m. At 9:00 a.m. it received orders to march back to Mukden, where it arrived, exhausted, at the railway station in the afternoon! Why this senseless marching back and forth took place, no one could say!

This disposition proves that the attacks ordered the previous day for the 5th were not carried out, and results in a shift of forces to the disadvantage of the attacking wing.

The mission of the reserve is incomprehensible.

The actual events of the day on the *western* wing, briefly summarized, were: on the Xinmintun Mandarin Road,¹) Xiujiachuan, about two kilometers from Dashiqiao, cannot be taken and the overall attack grinds to a halt.

For 7 March, either no attack disposition had been issued, or, on the basis of bad experiences with the two previous ones, it was preferred not to make one known to the foreign officers. On this day, fighting takes place west of Mukden near Niuxintun, Yangshitun, and Yuhongtun. The Russian forces employed here, employed in a unified manner, achieve local successes. Colonel Zapolsky falls at Bajiazi. General von der Launitz's group crosses to the northern bank of the Hun River in order to protect Mukden against the threat manifesting itself ever more forcefully from the north.

To characterize the chaos, here is the composition of a column encountered by one of the military attachés while riding away from Houhu: [[p316]] *two companies of Rifle Regiment No. 5* (hitherto with Lieutenant General Tserpitsky's group); between them, *Batteries Nos. 6 and 29* (8th Army Corps), and *a combined regiment* of the 4th Siberian Corps (soldiers questioned claimed to belong to the Irkutsk, Omsk, Tomsk, Tobolsk, and Barnaul regiments!).
