#### 1 March.

Thus, a *combined division* from the Tenth Army Corps (2nd Brigade of the 9th and 2nd Brigade of the 31st Division, with the 35th, 36th, 123rd, and 124th Infantry Regiments and artillery) was placed in readiness at General Headquarters near the railway.

General of Cavalry Baron *Kaulbars* stood west of Mukden with almost the entire Third Army: to reinforce him, Lieutenant General *Topornin* with the remainder of the Sixteenth Army Corps and, in addition, the new general army reserve, the combined division of the 10th Army Corps under General *Shatilov*, had to be deployed on 1 March in the direction of Salinpu.

On this day, Sifantai and the position on the Hunhe were attacked anew, with the enemy envelopment in the rear already making itself felt against Sifantai.

On the evening of the 1st, army headquarters decided to *pull back* the right wing on the Hunhe: specifically, the *Eighth Army Corps* in its position on the left bank of the Hunhe was to be *relieved by the Rifle Corps* and itself likewise march to Salinpu.

#### 2 March.

Besides "combining," the "*relieving*" of troops engaged in combat was also very popular with the Russian General Staff. [[p307]] It failed *regularly*—and so, of course, this time as well: the riflemen evacuated their position, the Eighth marched off *before* being relieved, the enemy pressed forward, and the entire sector on the Hunhe opposite Sandepu was lost. The Rifle Corps could only be saved through the intervention of the 241st and 215th Regiments near Lijiapu on 2 March.

On the same day, the *general reserve* that had arrived under Generals Topornin and Shatilov also intervened in the fighting. General Shatilov, with the combined division of the Tenth Corps, found Salinpu already occupied by the Japanese and came under artillery fire. To the east of him marched General Topornin; he attacked and brought to a halt the enemy advancing against Shatilov.

*Situation on the evening of 2 March:* On the Hunhe near Lijiapu, the *Rifle Corps*, then to the north of it the *Eighth Army Corps*, both with *badly intermingled units*. Opposite Salinpu, the columns of Shatilov and Topornin.

*The enemy* not only stood opposite this front, but by 2 March had already crossed the Xinmintun road; beyond it, in Xishelizhi, three Japanese regiments were reported.

The *Birger Brigade*, whose mission was to secure Mukden against enemy forces coming from the direction of the Xinmintun road, had been pushed northward and cut off after costly fighting near Dafangshen.
