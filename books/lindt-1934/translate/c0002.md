When our new horses were ready, the master of the merchants' guild presented us with two tins: the peaches bore the label: Made in U. S. A., and the fish came from Japan.

The hills ducked back into the plain, making room for the sky. (Taken, with the special permission of the publisher F. A. Brockhaus, Leipzig, from the just-published book *Im Sattel durch Mandschukuo* [In the Saddle Through Manchukuo] by A. R. Lindt.)
